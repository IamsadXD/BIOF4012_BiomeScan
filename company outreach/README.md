# Company outreach

Thirty unsent invitations organized into six weeks of five contacts, including eight farm operators. The messages use the approved BSc(Bioinformatics) and full Faculty of Medicine wording, with no suggested time slots. They ask about the current problem and workflow without introducing BiomeScan's solution.

## Copy and paste

1. Open the relevant weekly folder and its company `.txt` file.
2. Replace `[Full name]` in the introduction and signature, and `[Email]` in the signature. These details have not been provided, so no identity or sender address has been guessed.
3. Copy the first line after `Subject:` into your email subject, then copy the message below it into the email body or enquiry form. The company greeting and problem topic are already tailored.
4. Use the weekly README for the public contact route, role to request and known limitations. Those notes are deliberately outside the message so they will not be pasted into an invitation. For phone-only farms, first ask for their preferred business recipient.

The expected interview duration remains 15–20 minutes. Let recipients propose their availability. These are research invitations, not appointments or sent messages. Do not attach a pitch or product demo before the interview.
