## After an interview is accepted

Confirm the participant meets the sheet's eligibility criteria. Let the participant suggest a convenient time, then confirm the interview arrangements and language. The expected duration is 15–20 minutes. Ask for the correct consent route; attendance or an email reply is not recording consent. Do not add photos or video by default.
