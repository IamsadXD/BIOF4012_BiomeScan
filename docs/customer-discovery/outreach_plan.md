## Weekly routine

Personalize five requests using the directory and problem-first templates. For phone-only farms, first request the appropriate business recipient and permission to send the invitation. State the voluntary student customer-discovery purpose. Keep the solution out of recruitment and until the end of an interview.

After five working days, follow up once. A closing follow-up may be sent after a further five to seven working days, then stop. Respect refusals immediately. Record contacted, replied, booked and completed separately. If the rubric requires five completed interviews rather than five contacts, clarify how non-response should be documented and expand outreach honestly; sending five messages cannot guarantee completion.

Each week spans problem segments intentionally: compare findings within construction, pest control, property, ecology and farming rather than pooling them into one apparent market. Check which weeds actually matter to farms and whether finding them is harder than removal. Small production areas or adequate walking checks may make a farm a poor buyer even when it is a useful interview.

Use a restricted external outreach log with organization, role, first-contact date, template, follow-up date, outcome, interview code and permitted next step. Do not commit the live log or research records. The source consent materials promise anonymized reporting. Public contact details in this folder are prospecting information, not participant data.

## Counting and learning

Count one organization once per new-contact target. Multiple projects, branches, parent/subsidiary routes or referrals can share an end budget; confirm independence. Farm listings identify operating names, not incorporation. Confirm the course accepts commercial farm operators if its requirement literally says companies. Government and conservation conversations are additional stakeholders rather than substitutes for this schedule.

After ten interviews, compare recent recurring problems, quantified burdens, current alternatives, resource owners and purchasing routes within each segment. Record low-priority and satisfactory-process answers. A paid pilot or data reuse is a separate later agreement, not an implied consequence of a course interview.
