# BiomeScan company and farm directory

Prepared 3 November 2026. Thirty distinct business or farm-operator leads support six weeks of five new contacts per week. These are unsent research prospects, not customers. Eight are farms; their trading names and incorporation status are not interchangeable. Confirm independent operating entities and the course counting rule before counting farm operators as legal companies. Government and NGOs are separate stakeholders below.

Roles are suggested routing requests, not verified staff titles. Every lead needs qualification. Contact routes researched on 2 November are labelled with that date; new routes and farms were researched on 3 November. All routes are untested for delivery. Source dates and known retrieval/age limitations are recorded rather than implying current access to a named buyer.

The farms come from AFCD's [30 September 2026 directory](https://www.afcd.gov.hk/english/agriculture/LF/files/List_Agri_enJoy_Scheme_30092026.pdf), pages 1 and 2, linked by the [Agri enJoy scheme page](https://www.afcd.gov.hk/english/agriculture/LF/LF.html). The scheme concerns commercial crop-producing farms. Directory inclusion is a reason to ask about farm operations, not evidence of weed problems or survey demand. Phone first and ask whether an interview invitation is welcome; do not assume a published mobile number permits messaging. Production area, weeds, staffing and affordability must be established in the interview.
