# BiomeScan customer discovery

Prepared 3 November 2026. This folder contains the BiomeScan adaptations of the supplied BIOF4012 interview, information and consent materials, plus a six-week outreach plan. It covers invasive-plant management, farm weed inspection, and standing-water inspection as separate interview topics. No outreach has been sent and no customer demand is claimed.

## Three working documents

1. **Interview questions:** [Word](01_interview_questions.docx) and [editable text](01_interview_questions.md). Eight core questions and four optional questions preserve the sample's five themes. Any solution discussion comes at the end.
2. **Participant information and consent:** [Word](02_participant_information_and_consent.docx) and [editable text](02_participant_information_and_consent.md). Combines participant information, written consent and an oral script. This is an investigator-review draft; the unresolved retention and approval fields must be settled before participant use.
3. **Interviewer guidelines:** [Word](03_interviewer_guidelines.docx) and [editable text](03_interviewer_guidelines.md). Recruitment, interview conduct, sector probes, evidence handling and the original consent discrepancies.

## Outreach and market work

- [Company outreach folders](../../company%20outreach/README.md): one tailored
  text invitation per prospect, grouped into six weeks, with contact routes in
  each weekly index. Sender wording uses BSc(Bioinformatics) and the School of
  Biomedical Sciences name; no meeting times are proposed.
- [Six-week outreach plan](outreach_plan.md): five distinct businesses or farm operators each week, 30 in total, including eight farms.
- [Company and farm directory](company_directory.md): public routes, roles to request, qualification questions and source dates.
- [Problem-first emails](problem_first_emails.md): construction, pest control, ecology, property, government and farming versions, plus follow-ups.
- [Market assessment](market_assessment.md): evidence of existing work and the demand assumptions still to test.

The directory includes farm trading names, not a verified incorporation register. If the course requires incorporated companies rather than business operators, confirm its counting rule before using farms toward the quota. Government, conservation and NGO contacts are listed separately and do not inflate the 30-business schedule. Multiple teams within one organization are not independent organization counts or necessarily independent budgets.

Use the weekly plan and directory as the current schedule; it supersedes the 2 November draft order. Public routes are not delivery-tested. Prior contacts were researched on 2 November; added company routes and the September 2026 AFCD farm directory were checked on 3 November. Recheck routes before sending.

Do not commit completed consent forms, signatures, participant identities, correspondence, recordings, transcripts, interview notes, confidential site records or actual outreach logs to this repository. The documents contain placeholders and public organizational contact information only. Store research records through the investigator-approved arrangement.
