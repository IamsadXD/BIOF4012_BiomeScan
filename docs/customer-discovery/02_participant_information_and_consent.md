## Participant information

**Study title:** Customer Discovery and Validation Research for Biomedical Innovation and Entrepreneurship.

**Investigators:** Dr Masayo Kotaka and Mr Jeffrey Broer, School of Biomedical Sciences, The University of Hong Kong.

**Student co-investigators:** [Student names and HKU email].

**Selected interview topic:** [Choose one: organizational invasive-plant inspection and management; farm weed inspection and management; organizational standing-water and mosquito-prevention inspection].

This interview forms part of a student course project on customer discovery and entrepreneurship. We are examining whether organizations have unmet operational or business needs in the selected topic. Your experience will help us test our problem hypothesis and decide whether a business idea is worth pursuing. We are interested in satisfactory current practices as well as difficulties.

### Who may participate

Hong Kong residents aged 18 or above with relevant experience carrying out, overseeing or commissioning the selected task. A work role or business address alone does not establish residence. You are invited for your experience; the interview does not require an official statement or endorsement from your employer. Please obtain any workplace permission you need before sharing information.

### What taking part involves

A phone, online or in-person interview lasting approximately 15–20 minutes, with 8–12 questions about a recent example, current practices, difficulties, resources and how comparable work is approved. You may use broad time or cost ranges. Please avoid confidential client or resident information, specific enforcement cases, exact sensitive site locations and unpublished contract details. If within the study scope confirmed by the investigators, we may offer a brief explanation of the team's idea only after the problem questions; you may decline that discussion.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip questions or stop without giving a reason or suffering a penalty. There is no direct benefit promised. Some questions may concern commercially sensitive work; you may keep your answers general or decline them. Your contribution supports student learning about customer needs.

### Recording and confidentiality

Audio recording is optional and requires separate permission before it starts. It is used for accurate transcription. Names and identifying details will be removed or generalized in analysis and reporting, including distinctive job titles, organizations or site descriptions where these could identify you. We will not attribute your responses to your employer. Photographs and video are not requested in this version of the interview.

Recordings, transcripts and consent records will be held in password-protected storage accessible only to the investigators and authorized student co-investigators under the confirmed course arrangement. ## Oral consent script

For the student interviewer. Use only after the investigators confirm oral consent is appropriate and all unresolved fields in the information sheet have been completed. Read before starting any recording. Record the consent decision in the approved consent log; do not record a refusal on an audio file started without permission.

“Hello, my name is [name]. I am an HKU student conducting a course customer-discovery interview about [selected problem topic]. We are exploring whether there are unmet operational or business needs. We want to hear how your current process works, including what is satisfactory.

The interview takes about 15–20 minutes and covers 8–12 questions. Participation is voluntary. You may skip a question, stop, or withdraw before the interview or within two weeks afterward. Please avoid confidential workplace, client, resident or sensitive site information. We do not need an official statement from your employer.

With separate permission, we would like to audio-record for accurate transcription. Identifying details will be removed or generalized in analysis and reporting. You may review and request erasure of part or all of the recording. We will not take photos or video.

The records will be held securely under the confirmed course arrangement. [Read the exact investigator-confirmed retention and deletion statement from the information sheet.] Study questions can be directed to masayo@hku.hk, and participant-rights questions to hrec@hku.hk.

Have you received and understood the information sheet, and do you have any questions?”

Confirm separately, without pressure:

1. “Are you a Hong Kong resident aged 18 or above?”
2. “Do you consent to taking part in the interview?”
3. “Do you consent to audio recording for transcription?”
4. “May we contact you once afterward about this interview?”

Record the interview code, information-sheet version, date, interviewer and each answer separately in the restricted consent record. Begin the interview only after participation consent, and start audio only after separate recording consent. Give the participant their code and contact route for withdrawal.
